Amoji Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Amoji ("we") provides a virtual companion app with pet care, chat, and chase mini-games. This policy describes data we collect when you use our iOS, Android, and web apps.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers (guest ID, Apple Sign In subject ID, optional email)
  • Game save data (pet stats, coins, chase scores, settings)
  • Purchase receipts verified via Apple App Store / Google Play (via RevenueCat)
  • Optional voice/chat content when you use companion features (processed per session)

How we use data

  • Cloud save sync across your devices
  • Deliver companion chat, TTS, and pet/chase gameplay
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Improve stability (only if you opt in to analytics in Settings)

Third parties

We may use Apple Sign In, RevenueCat, Vercel hosting, and AI/voice providers configured in your deployment. Each service has its own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • Sign in as guest without email
  • Disable analytics in Settings
  • Delete account: contact support or sign out and request deletion
